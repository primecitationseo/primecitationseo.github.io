Local Citation Building

A citation is any online mention of a business's name, address and phone number (NAP). Search engines use citations, in part, to judge how consistent and trustworthy a business's information is across the web.

Prime Citations builds and maintains citations with a process that prioritizes accuracy over volume:

  • Accurate, consistent business information across every listing
  • Relevant general, industry-specific and location-specific directories
  • Manual duplicate-listing research and cleanup
  • Citation audits to identify inconsistent or outdated data
  • Quality checks on every submission before it's marked complete
  • Reporting with live URLs where a listing is publicly viewable

Citation building supports local search visibility as part of a broader SEO strategy; it does not guarantee a specific ranking position.

Link Building

Off-page SEO relies heavily on the quality and relevance of a website's link profile, not just the number of links pointing to it.

Link building work is approached with a focus on:

  • Relevant, topically-aligned backlink opportunities
  • Source authority and trustworthiness
  • A natural-looking, diversified link profile
  • Long-term SEO health over short-term volume

Not every backlink improves rankings automatically — results depend on relevance, competition and the receiving site's overall SEO profile.

Google Stacking & Cloud Stacking

Google stacking and cloud stacking involve creating and organizing supporting brand properties — such as documents, folders, sites and profiles on trusted platforms — that reinforce a business's entity and brand presence online.

This work focuses on:

  • Consistent brand information across supporting properties
  • Contextually relevant supporting content
  • Structured organization that's easy to maintain
  • Entity and brand presence signals

Google stacking and cloud stacking are supporting tactics within a broader SEO strategy, not a standalone or guaranteed ranking method.
